THE PROBLEM

Small problems can become big problems.

📞

Difficult Reporting

People may not know where or how to report everyday problems in their local area.

⏳

Lack of Visibility

After reporting an issue, people often have limited visibility into what happens next.

Issues Go Unnoticed

Problems in public spaces can remain unnoticed until they become more serious.

OUR SOLUTION

One simple place to make your community better.

FixNear creates a simple connection between people and the problems around them.

Users can submit an issue, provide its location, describe the problem and follow its progress.
